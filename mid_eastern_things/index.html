i hate the coalition provisional authority

The CPA or the Coalition Provisional Authority was a temporary government set-up after the overthrowing of Saddam Hussein in 2003 after Operation Iraqi Freedom. Now why I dislike the CPA so much is because of its sheer inability to understand the Iraqi social framework. The Neoconservatives' (Vice President Dick Cheney, Secretary of Defense Donald Rumsfeld, CPA Leader Paul Bremer. etc) idea of just removing a dictatorship (Ba'athist Iraq) and installing a democracy was going to work on paper. The paper is not the real world. The secterian divide between the majority Shia, and the minorities of Sunni and Kurds complicated this matter. Beginning with CPA Order No.1 (De-Ba'athification) which removed all Ba'ath party memebers from public employment, many of whom were teachers, medical personnel and technicians. Not if all of these people had Party membership because the government mandated them to and were ideologically opposed or negligent towards Ba'athism. This order paralyzed almost all Iraqi state institustions and services, including water and electricity. If this wasn't worse enough, CPA Order No.2 disbanded the entire Iraqi military, including the Intelligence Services and the Republican Guard. Leaving the approxiamtely 400,000 Iraqi soldiers and officers, many of whom were from the minority Sunni class were left unemployed and humiliated with no pay. This led to complete loss of civil order and looting after the invasion ended. These angry and now jobless soldiers and officers, including those form the elite Republical Guard joined the Insurgency within weeks and Al-Qaeda in Iraq which later mutated into ISIS. This leads to an another problem. The complete lack of Iraqi socials made the installment of a democratic government impossibe. The Shia majority will vote of a party that represents them, the Sunni and Kurds looking at this said "they will vote for their parties, we will never win an election" and they outright denounced and abandoned the system they had no say in creating. Eventually President Obama left Iraq in 2011, but had to return in 2014 during Operation Inherent Resolve to fight ISIS. Iraq was the sole country against Iran that was competent enough to be a regional Sunni block against the Shia majority Iran. Supporting Saddam was not a good thing but they had to because the US and Britain overthrew a good Iranian Democratic government in 1953 and installed the Shah Reza Pahlavi. The Shah was a brutal dictator who used SAVAK to keep his power, rightfully the Iranians managed to overthrow the Shah which became a sworn enemy of the United States as the Islamic Republic of Iran.
